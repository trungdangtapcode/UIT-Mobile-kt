# Asset provenance

- **`uit-emblem.png`** is the original 1967 × 1537 transparent standalone
  emblem from the [UIT logo bundle](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1cATZFagnPw8zjvP_cx8J1pDNtHRs3xgz).
  The supplied file is [Logo UIT_RutGon_Transparent.png](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1-biSDkEc-CmS8NVHScVTLl4v7e2M6L6P/view).
  The original PNG identifies the university in the seminar. UIT
  retains the rights to its emblem.
- **`fonts/BeVietnamPro-{Regular,SemiBold,Bold,ExtraBold}.ttf`** are the static
  Be Vietnam Pro fonts at weights 400, 600, 700 and 800. The regular italic
  accompanies the upright faces as **BeVietnamPro-Italic.ttf**. The original binaries
  are from the [Google Fonts pin](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/83c62e30d87daa70216dab6b984d00d20d96d585/ofl/bevietnampro).
  The project authors license the fonts under the SIL Open Font License 1.1,
  reproduced in [`fonts/OFL.txt`](fonts/OFL.txt).
- **`fonts/IBMPlexMono-Regular.ttf`** is the static IBM Plex Mono font at weight
  400 from the [Google Fonts pin](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/83c62e30d87daa70216dab6b984d00d20d96d585/ofl/ibmplexmono).
  IBM licenses it under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, reproduced in
  [`fonts/IBM-Plex-Mono-OFL.txt`](fonts/IBM-Plex-Mono-OFL.txt).

The font binaries are bundled so the deck can build without a separate font
installation. The licenses and credits for external assets are recorded here
and in the provenance files alongside those assets.
